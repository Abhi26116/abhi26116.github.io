Privacy Policy

Startup Merge: Idle Tycoon

All apps Startup Merge Support Privacy

Effective November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information Startup Merge (“the game”, “we”) handles and why. In short: the game itself does not collect or send your personal information to us. The advertising partner, Google AdMob, collects some data to show ads, as described below.

Your game progress

Your progress (coins, items, gems and settings) is saved on your device. It is also saved to your own iCloud account through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it can be restored on a new iPhone. We cannot see or access this data. It is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. The game only checks Apple's signed receipt on your device to give you what you bought.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads (for example, “Watch Ad: 2X Profit”). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and limit how often you see the same ad, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location
  • Ad interactions, such as views and taps
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information

Google's use of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether the game may track you. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized with your advertising identifier.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): you are asked for consent before ads use your data. You can change your choice in the game under Settings (⚙️) → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • No ads at all: watching ads is always optional. The “No Ads” purchase lets you start the boost without watching one.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders (for example, when your daily reward is ready) on your device. They are not sent through any server, and you can turn them off in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Children

Startup Merge is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting data

Deleting the app removes your progress from the device. To remove the copy in iCloud, go to iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage. Because we do not hold any of your personal data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; for data held by Google, see the links above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its use. Since the game does not send your data to us, most requests are best made to Google or Apple using the links above. You can also contact us below and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

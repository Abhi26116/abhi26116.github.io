Every billion-dollar company starts somewhere. Yours starts with a cup of coffee.

Merge two coffees into an idea, two ideas into a business plan, and keep going until you build a unicorn. Along the way venture firms pitch you term sheets: sell a share of your company to grow faster, or bootstrap and keep it all. The shares you keep decide how many investors stay with you when you take the company public.

Then start again in the next city, from Silicon Valley to London, Tokyo and Mars.

Free to play on iPhone and iPad, with optional in-app purchases and optional rewarded ads.

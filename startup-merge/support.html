Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Drag an item onto an identical one to merge them into the next item. Every item on the board earns coins each second. Build your city's final item, then take the company public with an IPO. In the game, open Settings (⚙️) → How to Play, or tap Replay the Tutorial.

My board is full. What now?

Merge pairs to free up space, deliver items to clients, or drag items into storage (the row under the board). Stored items don't earn, and more slots can be unlocked with gems.

I bought something but didn't get it.

Open Settings (⚙️) → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com; Apple handles all payments.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Yes. Progress is saved to your iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple Account and keep iCloud turned on (iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud), then install the game. Your progress loads when it opens.

The “Watch Ad” button doesn't play an ad.

Sometimes no ad is available. Check your internet connection and try again in a minute. In some regions ads also need your consent first; you can review it in Settings (⚙️) → Ad Privacy Choices.

How do I turn off sound, vibration or reminders?

Open Settings (⚙️) in the top-right corner of the game.

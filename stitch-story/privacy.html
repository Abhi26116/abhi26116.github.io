Privacy Policy

Stitch Story: Thread Sort Puzzle

All apps Stitch Story Support Privacy Terms

Effective November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information Stitch Story (“the game”, “we”) handles and why. In short: your game progress stays on your device. Our advertising and analytics partners collect some data, as described below.

Your game progress

Your progress, coins, Hearts, finished pictures, gallery and settings are saved on your device only. The game has no account, no chat and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your name, payment details or store account. The game only receives a receipt for the item bought.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads between levels and optional rewarded ads (for example “Watch ad: +1 Slot”). Ads are provided by Google AdMob and the ad partners Google works with. To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and limit how often you see the same ad, they may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS only if you allow tracking; the Android advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location
  • Device and app information, such as model, OS version and language
  • Ad interactions, such as views and taps

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

The game uses Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config to understand which levels are too hard, fix crashes and adjust game settings. This sends anonymous game events (for example “level started”, “level completed”), app version, device model, OS version, country and crash reports. It does not include your name or contact details. See Firebase privacy and security.

New albums

The game downloads new picture albums from our website (hosted by GitHub Pages). Like any website, the host sees your IP address for the request. We do not store it.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): you are asked for consent at the first start before ads use your data. You can change your choice in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Tracking (iOS): iOS asks whether the game may track you. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized with your advertising identifier.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings.
  • No ads: rewarded ads are always optional, and the Remove Ads purchase turns off ads between levels.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders (for example, when your lives are full or the daily picture is ready) on your device, at most one a day. They are not sent through any server, and you can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Children

Stitch Story is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting data

Settings → Delete my data, or deleting the app, removes your progress from the device. Analytics data is kept by Firebase for up to 14 months. Because we do not hold any personal data ourselves, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; for data held by Google or Apple see the links above, or contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the CCPA or India's DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its use. We do not sell personal information. Contact us below and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

All apps Stitch Story Support Privacy Terms

Stitch Story can be played without an account. If you signed in with Apple or Google to save your progress in the cloud, you can delete that cloud account at any time.

Delete it in the game

  1. Open Stitch Story and go to Settings → Saved progress → Cloud account.
  2. Tap Manage, then Delete cloud account, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Apple or Google when asked.

This deletes right away your cloud account (the account identifier and, for Google sign-in, the email address stored with it) and the copy of your game progress in it. For Sign in with Apple, the game's access to your Apple account is revoked. The progress on your phone stays until you delete it with Settings → Delete my data, which also removes the backup copies.

If the game is no longer installed, install it, sign in with the same Apple or Google account under Settings → Saved progress, and delete it as above.

Ask us to delete it

You can also email tinygeniushubapp@gmail.com with the subject “Stitch Story: delete my account”. Tell us whether you used Apple or Google; for Google, write from the email address of that Google account. We delete the account and its copy within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is kept

Nothing from your cloud account is kept after deletion. Anonymous analytics and crash reports (without your name or contact details) are kept by Firebase for up to 14 months, as described in the Privacy Policy. Purchases are kept by Apple or Google as part of your store account.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap a free spool in the pile. It goes onto a needle and stitches every open cell of its colour, from the outside of the picture inwards. A spool whose colour cannot be stitched yet waits on the shelf, which has 5 slots. Finish the picture to win; if the shelf overflows the thread gets tangled.

How do lives work?

You lose a life only when you give up a tangled level. One life comes back every 30 minutes, up to 5. Infinite lives from the Shop let you play without losing any.

I bought something but didn't get it.

Open Settings or the Shop and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with. Remove Ads and premium albums come back on any device. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com (iPhone/iPad) or Google Play's order history (Android); the stores handle all payments.

I deleted the game. Is my progress gone?

No. Install it again on the same phone (iPhone: with the same Apple ID and iCloud on; Android: with the phone's Google backup on): the game greets you with “Welcome back!” and “Continue level …”.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Yes, if you sign in: Settings → Saved progress → Cloud account → Sign in with Apple or Google, then sign in with the same account on the new phone, iPhone or Android. Purchases that last (Remove Ads, premium albums) can also be restored with Restore Purchases.

How do I change ad privacy choices or delete my data?

Settings → Privacy choices changes your ad consent. Settings → Delete my data removes all game data from the device and its backups. To delete a cloud account, see Delete your account and the Privacy Policy.

How do I turn off sound, music, vibration, reminders or change the language?

Open Settings (the gear on the Home screen) or pause a level.
